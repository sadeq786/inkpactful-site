Let's see if Inkpactful is the right fit

Tell us what you are trying to communicate, why it matters now, and where you need support.

During this brief 15-minute call, we will learn more about the project, answer any initial questions, and determine whether Impact Lens™ is the right next step.

There is no pressure and no free consulting disguised as a sales call. The goal is simply to understand the need and decide whether it makes sense to move forward together.

What we'll discuss

We will ask about:

  • The communication you are considering
  • The audience it needs to reach
  • Why the project matters now
  • Any deadlines or funding moments involved
  • What information and materials are currently available
  • Who will be involved in the project and decision

You do not need to have everything figured out before the call. You may already know exactly what you need, or you may simply know that an important reporting, stewardship, or fundraising moment is approaching.

What happens next

If the project appears to be a strong fit, we will invite you to begin with Impact Lens.

Impact Lens is a focused 90-minute process that clarifies the purpose, audience, narrative, evidence, gaps, and direction for one important impact communication.

You will receive a written brief with clear strategic direction for moving the project forward.

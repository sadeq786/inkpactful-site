Impact communications that help people understand what your work made possible

Your organization is doing meaningful work. But the information needed to communicate that impact may be spread across reports, spreadsheets, staff members, interviews, financial documents, and stories from the field.

Inkpactful brings those pieces together into clear, credible, and compelling communications that strengthen trust with donors, funders, partners, and supporters.

How we can help

Annual Reports

Bring your organization's full year into one cohesive account of its progress, priorities, financial stewardship, and impact.

We help transform scattered results, perspectives, and supporting evidence into an annual report that gives donors and stakeholders a clear understanding of what their support made possible and why the work matters.

Impact Reports

Show what a particular grant, program, campaign, restricted fund, initiative, or major investment accomplished.

Impact reports help you meet an important reporting or stewardship moment while strengthening the larger relationship behind it. The work may involve clarifying the intended audience, identifying the strongest evidence and stories, and shaping the information into a focused, persuasive account of impact.

Donor and Funder Communications

Keep supporters connected to the work between major reporting moments.

We help nonprofits develop thoughtful communications that show progress, reinforce the value of continued support, and make donors and funders feel informed rather than contacted only when another contribution is needed.

This may include newsletters, stewardship updates, campaign communications, and other donor-facing materials.

Impact Messaging and Narrative

Clarify how your organization explains what it does, why it matters, and what changes because of the work.

We help bring greater consistency and purpose to the way leadership and staff communicate impact across audiences and channels. The result is a stronger foundation for reports, fundraising conversations, donor communications, presentations, and future campaigns.

Case Studies and Stakeholder Stories

Show the human significance behind the numbers without losing substance or credibility.

Through thoughtful interviews and narrative development, we help capture the experiences of participants, partners, staff members, donors, and other stakeholders in ways that illustrate the broader impact of your work.

Brochures and Fundraising Communications

Give prospective supporters a clear and persuasive introduction to your mission, impact, and case for support.

We help nonprofits shape brochures and other fundraising materials around what their audience most needs to understand, rather than simply compressing existing information into another piece of collateral.

Not sure which service fits?

You may already know exactly what you need. Or you may know there is an important reporting, stewardship, or fundraising moment ahead without knowing the best form the communication should take.

Every engagement begins with Impact Lens™, a focused process that clarifies the purpose, audience, narrative, evidence, and direction for one important impact communication.
